# Vision 01 — The Hollow Vial

Shot script for task **18a** (Viki). The vision that plays when Bunk smells
`clue_01` in the droggery.

Style is fixed by [vision-style-guide.md](vision-style-guide.md): `#120417`
black, `#ffffff` white, `#b60c28` red, min 10px line, Ink-3 Gpen, hard split.
This is an **object-hint**, so red fills the thing that matters — the same rule
as test frame 01, the wine glass.

Six panels, roughly **7 seconds** total.

---

## The rule this script obeys

**Everything shown is true, and the obvious reading of it is wrong.**

The player has already been told by Barbara that a human cannot swallow the
Black Wind — it kills on the tongue. That is true. It is also why nobody looks
for someone who swallowed it. This vision shows the theft exactly as it
happened and lets the player conclude the medicine was *poured out somewhere*,
because they are reasoning from a fact that does not apply to a mutant.

Nobody is cheated. They are handed the answer and their own knowledge talks
them out of it.

---

## Panel 1 — THE ROOM · 1.0s

Wide, low, shot **from where the vial sits on the shelf**. Bottles in the
foreground as vertical black bars, simplified, no labels. Beyond them the shop:
the edge of the counter, the suggestion of people standing — dark masses only,
no features.

- **No red at all.**
- White is the shop's artificial light on the bottle shoulders.

*Purpose:* orient the player. They should recognise the room they are standing
in, and understand they are watching from the shelf.

## Panel 2 — THE HAND · 1.0s

Mid shot. A hand enters from frame right, reaching between the bottles. Dark
sleeve cuff, thin wrist, knuckles drawn old — prominent, dry, careful.

- Flat black silhouette with a white rim-light along the top edge.
- **Still no red.**

*Purpose:* a person, but only a hand. The nose gave Bunk the gesture, not a
face, and the framing should feel like that limit rather than like coyness.

## Panel 3 — THE VIAL · 1.2s

Close. The hand closes around a small vial.

- **The vial is the only red in the frame.** The Black Wind inside it, dull and
  heavy. Everything else black and white.

*Purpose:* this is the medicine. A fortune, and a cure, in a bottle.

## Panel 4 — THE TILT · 1.5s

**The pivot of the whole case. Crop it deliberately.**

The vial tipped. Red in motion — streaks, a lean of liquid. Frame it so you
**cannot see where the contents go**: cut above the wrist and below whatever the
vial is tilting toward. No mouth, no vessel, no aquarium, no floor.

*Purpose:* the player fills the gap themselves, and fills it wrongly. Do not
resolve this shot. If a viewer can tell what happened here, the level is over.

## Panel 5 — EMPTY · 1.0s

The vial upright again, still held. Clear glass.

- **The red is gone.** A thin smear left on the inside.

*Purpose:* the theft has already happened, and it happened in the cut. The
previous two panels trained the eye to follow red, so its absence should land
as a small shock.

## Panel 6 — PUT BACK · 1.3s

The hand returning the vial to the shelf, sliding it in among the others.
Fingers releasing. Unhurried. Pull wide enough that it joins the row and stops
being findable.

- A hairline of red left in the glass — the last red in the vision.

*Purpose:* the emotional beat, and the clue. It was never carried out of the
room. The final image is the vial *sitting there*, which is exactly what Bunk
finds when he looks.

---

## Continuity

- Same hand, same sleeve, all six panels.
- **Never draw above the wrist.** No arm, no shoulder, no body.
- The shelf line and bottle spacing in panel 6 match panel 1.

## Must not appear

Face · hair · body · height · gender · species · the mask · any other customer
in focus · anything that resolves panel 4.

## A note on the red budget

The style guide asks for roughly 18–22% red per frame. This sequence
deliberately does not hold that per panel: panels 1–2 have none, 3–4 carry it,
5–6 almost none. **The appearance and disappearance of red is the story here** —
the medicine arriving and going.

Leta, this is your guide, so overrule it if you would rather the budget hold
frame by frame. Flagging rather than quietly deviating.

## Audio hooks (task 22, later)

Glass against glass. A cork. And in the cut at panel 4, one soft sound the
player will not identify as a swallow until the second playthrough.
