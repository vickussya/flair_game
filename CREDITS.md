# Credits and third-party material

Everything in this repository that we did not make ourselves, and the terms it
came under. Keep this current — for the diploma submission it has to be
accurate, and a licence we cannot show is a licence we cannot use.

The placeholder register in [docs/tasks.md](docs/tasks.md) tracks which of these
are due to be replaced with our own work.

## The novel

**FLAIR (Нюх)** by **Emil Minchev** — the world, Bunk Romero, Barbara, Unterstadt,
the Church of the Suffering and the case this level adapts all come from the
novel. This is a student project based on it.

> **Permission is not yet arranged.** `docs/world.md` Part 2 records the intent to
> contact the author once the first level is finished. Until that happens, nothing
> here may be published, sold, or distributed beyond coursework and examiners.
> This is the single biggest open question in the project.

## Placeholder character and animation

**Mixamo** (Adobe) — the temporary Bunk body and the `Bunk_Idle`, `Bunk_Walk` and
`Bunk_Sniff` clips.

- Used under Mixamo's terms for use in a project.
- Mixamo does **not** allow redistributing its characters or animations as
  standalone asset files, and we cannot sublicense them. They sit in this
  repository as working files only.
- All of it is replaced by our own modelled, rigged and animated characters
  before submission.
